# Reusable prompts

Replace placeholders with real paths or task IDs. Supply linked files when the assistant cannot access the repository. Prompts invoke the authoritative guides rather than restating their rules.

For product discovery, architecture selection, delivery kickoff, and resumption, use the prompts in [README.md](../README.md).

## Prepare the backlog

> Read `AGENTS.md`. Act as PM under `_docs/team/pm.md` to prepare the backlog for the agreed specification.

## Groom a task

> Read `AGENTS.md`. Act as PM under `_docs/team/pm.md` to groom <task>.

## Implement a task

> Read `AGENTS.md`. Act as the developer under `_docs/team/software-engineer.md` for <ready task>.

## Verify a task

> Read `AGENTS.md`. Act as QA under `_docs/team/qa-engineer.md` for <task and revision>.

## Change a requirement

> Read `AGENTS.md`. Assess <proposed change> under the change policy in `_docs/process.md` before updating the agreed specification.
